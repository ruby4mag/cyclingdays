Intervals.icu connection

Your rides, wellness and calendar come from Intervals.icu, and the coach puts your workouts on its calendar.

On intervals.icu, open Settings and scroll to Developer Settings: copy the athlete ID and the API key (generate one if there isn't any). The key is stored encrypted and only used to read your data and add workouts.

Athlete profile

The coach sizes every workout from your FTP; it's prefilled from Intervals.icu when it has one.

Goal and training plan

The long-term plan: 4-week blocks building toward your event, with peak and taper weeks before it. Each evening the coach plans the next day inside it.

Your week

Key days carry the hard session; long days the longest ride. At least one rest day, at most two key days, never two key days in a row.

Email

After planning each evening, the coach can email you tomorrow's workout.

Disconnect Intervals.icu?

The coach stops planning and publishing workouts for you. Your stored history stays; connect again any time.
